PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

RECLUSE is a clothing brand based in Abuja, Nigeria, run by John Nafuh. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you shop on this website, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you place an order we ask for:

  • your full name
  • your email address
  • your phone number
  • your delivery address (not needed for pickup)
  • the items, sizes and delivery option you choose

We never see or store your card or bank details. Payments are handled by Paystack.

How we use it

  • to process and deliver your order
  • to contact you about your order, delivery or returns
  • to send you a payment receipt
  • to keep records of our sales

Who we share it with

We only share information with the services needed to run your order:

  • Paystack, which processes your payment. Their privacy policy applies to your payment details.
  • Formspree, which delivers your order details to us by email.
  • a delivery rider or courier, who receives only your name, phone number and address.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Your cart on this device

Your cart is saved in your own browser so it is still there if you leave and come back. It is not sent to us until you pay.

Keeping your information safe

We keep your order details only as long as we need them for your order, returns and our records, and we take reasonable steps to protect them. We handle personal information in line with Nigerian data protection law.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Message us on WhatsApp and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
